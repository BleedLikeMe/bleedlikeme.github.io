Controls & Guide

Ops Room

Click an incident marker on the radar, or a card in the sidebar, to open dispatch options — pick the best-matched, available asset and confirm. Use the speed controls (top right) to pause or fast-forward time between incidents.

Station

Repair and refuel your fleet, buy new vessels and mountain rescue teams, hire and assign crew, send crew on training courses, fit vessel upgrades, and open new bases along the Firth to cut response times to distant incidents. Run fundraising events from the Overview tab to top up the budget.

Assets

RIBs and All-Weather Lifeboats respond to incidents at sea; the Search & Rescue Helicopter can reach either sea or land incidents fastest; the Mountain Rescue Team responds to cliff and hillwalking incidents on land and can't put to sea. An asset that takes catastrophic damage is written off and must be replaced.

Major Incidents & Mutual Aid

Some large incidents (⚠ marked "MAJOR" on the board) need two assets on scene together before they can be resolved — dispatch one, then dispatch a second from the same incident's card. If your own fleet can't cover a call, Request Mutual Aid from a neighbouring station for a fee instead.

Storm Seasons

Every so often the forecast turns: a storm season brings several days of worse weather and more frequent incidents. It's announced in the log and shown on the Overview tab — plan crew rest and repairs around it where you can.
