Blackjack Rules

Beat the dealer by finishing closer to 21 without going over. Number cards count face value, court cards 10, and aces 1 or 11. An ace and a ten-value card as your first two cards is a Blackjack.

Hints & counting

Strategy hints highlight the basic-strategy play for these rules: the mathematically best decision given your hand and the dealer's up card. Hi-Lo count keeps a running count (2–6 count +1, 7–9 count 0, tens and aces count −1). The true count is the running count divided by the decks left in the shoe; a high true count favours the player.
