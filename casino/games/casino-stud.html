Casino Stud Poker

Also called Caribbean Stud. You play your five-card poker hand against the dealer's; there's no drawing and no bluffing. A fresh single deck is shuffled for every hand.

How a hand plays

1. Place an ante, and optionally the $1 jackpot bet. 2. You and the dealer each get five cards; one dealer card is face up. 3. Fold and lose the ante, or raise by betting exactly twice the ante. 4. The dealer reveals. The dealer must qualify with Ace-King high or better.

$1 progressive jackpot

A royal flush wins the whole jackpot, a straight flush 10% of it, four of a kind $500, a full house $100 and a flush $50. It pays on your own five cards whether or not the dealer qualifies, but you must raise (not fold) to collect. The meter grows with every jackpot bet, including from other tables.

Strategy

Raise with any pair or better, and fold anything below Ace-King. With Ace-King high, raise if one of your cards matches the dealer's up card, or if you hold a Queen or Jack. Played this way, the ante bet carries a house edge of about 5.2%.
