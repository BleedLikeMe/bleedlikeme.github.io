Payouts & Rules

The shooter's first roll is the come-out. 7 or 11 wins the Pass Line, 2/3/12 is craps. Any 4, 5, 6, 8, 9 or 10 becomes the point (the puck flips ON). The shooter keeps rolling: hit the point again and Pass wins; roll a 7 first ("seven out") and Pass loses.

Controls

Click a chip denomination, then click the layout. Right-click (or use Remove mode on touch screens) to take a bet down. To add odds behind a Come or Don't Come bet, click its chips on the number. Winning multi-roll bets stay up; one-roll bets also stay up until they lose. Payouts are rounded down to the cent.
