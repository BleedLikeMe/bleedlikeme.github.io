Punto Banco Rules

Two hands are dealt: Player (Punto) and Banker (Banco). You bet on which will finish closer to 9, or on a tie. Cards are worth face value, aces 1, and tens and court cards 0. Only the last digit of the total counts, so 7 + 8 = 15 counts as 5. Neither hand makes any decisions; the dealer draws strictly by the rules below.

The shoe & roads

Eight decks are shuffled into the shoe. The first card is turned up and that many cards (tens and court cards count as 10) are burned. The cut card sits 14 cards from the end; when it's reached, the shoe is reshuffled before the next hand. The Bead Plate records every result in order. The Big Road groups streaks into columns: red for Banker, blue for Player, a green slash for ties, and small dots for pairs.
