Sigil Crafting

Write a statement of intent ("I will find courage," "My home is protected"). Repeated letters and vowels are stripped away, and the remaining consonants are traced as a single line across a wheel of the alphabet — the classic chaos-magic technique for turning a sentence into a personal, abstract sigil.
