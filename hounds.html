Fair book: the prices are each dog's true chance of winning, and they add up to exactly 100%. The result is drawn using those same chances, so there's no bookmaker's margin. Each-way means two bets of your stake, one to win and one to place. The place part pays at ¼ of the odds if the dog finishes 1st or 2nd.

Forecast / Tricast

A forecast picks 1st and 2nd in the right order, and a tricast picks 1st, 2nd and 3rd. Pick more than one dog in a row (or tick Any order) to cover every combination; each line costs your stake from the box above. Payouts are the exact fair odds of that finishing order, worked out from the same chances as the win prices.
