THEIR FINEST HOUR

The Northern Sectors · 1940

France is falling. The last of the army is coming home from Dunkirk, and Norway will soon be in German hands. That puts Luftflotte 5's bombers at Stavanger, three hundred miles across the North Sea from your coast.

You are the Air Officer Commanding the northern fighter sectors, with your headquarters in Glasgow. Your squadrons guard Scapa Flow and the Home Fleet, the Forth and Rosyth, the Clyde shipyards, Rolls-Royce at Hillington, and the ports and airfields from Tyneside to Orkney.

The battle for Britain will be decided in the south, and Dowding knows it. When 11 Group starts to bleed, he will ask for your best squadrons. Send too few and the Luftwaffe wins air superiority over Kent, and the invasion comes. Send too many and the raiders from Norway get through to the Clyde. Your job is to hold both.

Squadron dispositions, radar sites and dates are researched but simplified. The Glasgow command is a slight fictionalisation, since the real 13 Group was run from Newcastle.
