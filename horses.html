Fair book: the prices are each horse's true chance of winning, and they add up to exactly 100%. The result is drawn using those same chances, so there's no bookmaker's margin. Each-way means two bets of your stake, one to win and one to place. With eight runners the place part pays at ⅕ of the odds if the horse finishes 1st, 2nd or 3rd. Over jumps, only horses that were never going to place can fall, unseat or be pulled up, so every price stays exact.

Forecast / Tricast

A forecast picks 1st and 2nd in the right order, and a tricast picks 1st, 2nd and 3rd. Pick more than one horse in a row (or tick Any order) to cover every combination; each line costs your stake from the box above. Payouts are the exact fair odds of that finishing order, worked out from the same chances as the win prices.
