How This Works

You're the Chief Race Engineer (or, in Cockpit view, the driver). Here's the short version:

Tyres wear down and go off a cliff past ~85%. Softer compounds are faster but wear quicker, and rain tyres only work in the wet.

Fuel burns every lap. Rich mode is faster but thirstier; Save is slower but stretches the tank. Running dry is a genuine emergency — heavy time penalty and a real chance of retiring.

ERS is a deployable power boost (Hotlap mode) that recharges in Save/Balanced modes.

Team Radio will interrupt with timed decisions — tyre calls, weather gambles, battles, pit-lane risk. Answer with the buttons or press 1/2 on your keyboard. If you don't answer, the safe default is taken automatically.

Qualifying sets your grid slot from one flying lap — tyre choice and how hard you push both matter.

Full Season mode carries points, a teammate rivalry record, and power-unit wear (which can trigger a grid penalty) across multiple races.
